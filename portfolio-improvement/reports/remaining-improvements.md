# Remaining improvements

## Needs external input

1. **Live demos and public repositories.** OdemLab is private; not every project has a public demo.
2. **Verified metrics.** Real test counts, coverage, latency or business results can be published only with evidence.
3. **OdemLab attribution.** The mobile app and AI diagnosis are teammates' work and must stay labelled that way.
4. **Dedicated case-study pages.** The current expandable case studies and shareable hashes cover the need; full pages would improve SEO and depth further.
5. **Independent visual score.** A designer should review the rendered site at desktop and mobile sizes.
6. **Lighthouse and axe measurements.** Run them in the deployment environment before claiming scores.

## Not planned without evidence

- Fabricated architecture diagrams.
- Fake dashboards or stock screenshots presented as project evidence.
- Claims of AI, payments, test volumes or ownership that are not in the final CVs.
